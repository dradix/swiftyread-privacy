Privacy Policy for SwiftyRead

Last updated: 9 August 2026

This policy explains what SwiftyRead ("the app") does with your information. It is written to describe the app as it actually behaves, not as a generic template.

SwiftyRead is provided by Alberto Viveros Delgado ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at jesus.viveros.delgado@gmail.com.

The short version

SwiftyRead has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. The documents you read and your reading progress stay on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

The one exception is advertising: the app shows banner ads supplied by Google AdMob, and the ads system collects information about your device in order to serve and measure those ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before any of that happens, and you can change your answer at any time.

What the app stores on your device

All of the following is written to your device's private app storage. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else, because the app has no server to transmit it to.

Your library. For each document you open, the app stores a reference to that file (an Android Storage Access Framework URI), its display name, your current word position, the total word count, and the time you last opened it.

Your preferences. Reading font and weight, highlight colour, theme, default reading speed, and punctuation pause settings.

Your documents themselves are never copied. The app holds a permission to read the file where it already lives; it does not duplicate it into app storage. PDF text extraction happens entirely on your device.

All of this is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

Information collected for advertising

The app displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google collects information from your device, which may include:

  • your advertising ID (a resettable identifier Android provides for this purpose)
  • your IP address, and an approximate location inferred from it
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, and the app you are using
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped

Google acts as an independent controller of this information. Its handling is governed by:

  • Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not receive this information ourselves. We see only aggregate, anonymous performance reports in the AdMob dashboard, such as total impressions and revenue. Those reports do not identify you.

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form the first time you open it, using Google's User Messaging Platform. No advertising request is made and the advertising SDK is not started until you have answered.

  • If you consent, you may be shown personalised ads.
  • If you do not consent, you may be shown non-personalised ads, or no ads at all, depending on your choices.

Outside those regions, ads are shown without a consent prompt, in line with local law.

Your legal basis, and how to change your mind

Where consent is required, the legal basis for advertising is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw or change it at any time:

  • Settings → Privacy options inside the app reopens the consent form, so you can revise any earlier answer. This appears wherever a consent form applies to you.
  • Android settings → Privacy → Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising ID, which prevents it being used to build a profile.
  • Clearing the app's storage resets your recorded consent, so you will be asked again next launch.

What we do not do

To be explicit, SwiftyRead does not:

  • require an account, email address, or any sign-in
  • collect analytics or usage statistics
  • collect crash reports
  • read, upload, index or transmit the contents of your documents
  • request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage-wide permissions
  • sell or share your information with anyone

The only network connections the app makes are those made by the Google advertising and consent SDKs.

Data retention

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data stored on your device persists until you remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's own policies, linked above.

Children

SwiftyRead is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will act on it.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability over personal data relating to you.

Because we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about information the app stores are satisfied by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. For information collected by Google for advertising, exercise your rights directly with Google using the links above, or withdraw consent in the app.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Google may process advertising data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Those transfers are covered by Google's own safeguards, described in its privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected in the app listing.
